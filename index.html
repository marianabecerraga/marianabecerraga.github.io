01 · Experiences

Festival Cordillera × Páramo Lab

FARO

Lighthouses to find your people again at a music festival.

The problem

A festival is one of the most connected places there is, until the network fails. Signal drops, phones die, and the people you came with dissolve into the crowd.

Insight

The fear isn't getting lost. It's never finding each other again.

Concept

When technology stops connecting people, the landscape has to. FARO is a family of lighthouses, one colour per meeting point: a sculpture by day, a beacon by night. Lost your group? Meet at the blue one.

Role
Concept development & visual direction
Team
4, with architecture students
Type
Academic proposal
Presented to
Páramo Lab
Next project Jardines Exquisitos →
Why the páramo

Nature, beacon, signal.

FARO grew from three silhouettes: the puya of the Colombian páramo, the lighthouse and the signal tower. The páramo is a quiet network of its own: it gathers the mist and slowly feeds the rivers below, keeping everything around it alive and connected.

For a proposal presented to Páramo Lab, that became the logic. Each lighthouse abstracts a páramo plant and takes its colour from it: four plants, four meeting points, one natural system for finding your way back to each other.

01 · Experiences · Thesis

Solo · 2025

Entre
el medio

Paintings and projections that only come alive when you stand still.

The pause

In a digital world where everything happens fast, this one asks for the opposite: only when you stand still does the digital art appear.

Concept

A circular path where the hand and the screen meet with no hierarchy between them. Motion sensors read the visitor: move, and you return to the painting; stop, and it wakes up.

Role
Concept, painting, drawing and interaction
Team

02 · Art direction

E://98 × La Cantaleta · 2025

Encarta 98

Visual worlds for two songs by E://98: a visualizer and a music video.

Desvanecer · Concept to image

A song about what fades. We built it on nostalgia: the present is a cold, late-night blue, a man alone in his room; the memory of the band arrives in warm, grainy light. Two temperatures for two times, and the feeling of something slipping away between them.

Calabazas · Concept to image

A return. The visualizer was conceived as the band presenting itself again after a long time away: one warm light in total darkness, bringing each member back into view, like faces surfacing from memory onto the stage.

Role
Creative and art direction

06 · Studio

Drawing · Painting · Observation

Studio

Before I designed images, I learned to look at them.

I'm a designer whose foundation is fine art. Years of drawing and painting from observation, in graphite, pastel and oil, taught me how light builds form, how a composition holds together and how a surface can carry a story.

That training is still how I see when I direct an image. And the practice continues: in summer 2026 I studied portrait painting from life at the Sacred Art School in Florence.
